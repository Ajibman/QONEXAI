QONEXAI Intelligence Layers

Introduction

The QONEXAI Intelligence Layers define how reasoning, analysis, behavior modeling, and geopolitical awareness are structured within the ecosystem.

These layers sit above infrastructure and below application domains, forming the cognitive core of the entire platform.

They are designed to evolve continuously through research-origin inputs, particularly from QuantumTrader-AI.

---

Layered Intelligence Philosophy

QONEXAI intelligence is not a single model or system.

It is a stack of interacting cognitive layers, each responsible for a different dimension of understanding and decision-making.

The goal is to separate:

- raw reasoning
- behavioral interpretation
- geopolitical context
- research-driven innovation
- domain execution

into a unified intelligence system.
